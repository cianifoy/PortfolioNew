Glossier App

A mobile app concept for Glossier that deepens community engagement, enhances shopping, and stays true to the brand’s values of inclusivity, simplicity, and digital-first connection.

Role
End-to-end product design
Team
Independent concept project

Opportunity

An app enables personalized engagement through notifications, loyalty, and exclusive content—while streamlining purchase flows and unlocking richer personalization from behavioral data.

Process

Personas, user flows from browse to checkout, wireframes, a responsive design system with tokens, AR try-on exploration, rewards concepts, and usability testing shaped the final experience.

Reflection

This project challenged me to build a design system from scratch and refine auto layout and prototyping skills while staying faithful to Glossier’s brand identity.
