Dexter

Dexter simplifies mealtime management for caregivers of children with type 1 diabetes—bringing glucose data, insulin dosing, recipes, favorites, and carb tracking into one calm, connected experience.

Role
Wireframes, mockups, prototype, and final design
Team
Product Manager, Project Manager, UX Research, Impact Specialist, and Technical Architect

Problem

How might we streamline mealtime management for parents and caregivers of children who have type 1 diabetes?

Research

A survey of 25 caregivers and affinity mapping revealed stress around carb counting accuracy, burnout, need for personalized information, and tools for real-time glucose management.

Outcome

Within a six-week healthcare hackathon track, the team delivered a product demo and pitch grounded in research, personas, wireframes, prototyping, and usability testing.
