Gap Inc. Power Reviews

Improved Power Reviews across Gap Inc. brands by adding review search, helpfulness voting, and smarter sorting—so shoppers can find trustworthy feedback faster.

Role
UX Designer, Gap Inc.
Team
Gap Inc. — Navigation / Power Reviews

Context

During my tenure at Gap Inc., I focused on navigation across Gap, Athleta, Banana Republic, and Old Navy—including search, SEO, filtering, and sorting.

Solution

Enhancements included a brand-aligned review search box, helpfulness voting with on-brand thumb icons, and sorting by most helpful so useful UGC rises to the top.

Why it matters

Gap Inc. aims to stay authentic with customers. Letting shoppers search terms like “fit” or “comfort” makes purchase decisions clearer and builds trust.
