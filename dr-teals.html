Dr Teal's Package Design

A redesign of Dr. Teal’s Lavender Foaming Bath packaging to embody the product essence and appeal to a more youthful audience while expanding the brand’s demographic reach.

Role
Package redesign
Team
Independent project

Goal

Elevate the packaging so it feels luxurious, relaxing, and approachable—matching what customers say they feel when they use the product.

Research

Amazon reviews surfaced keywords like luxurious, relaxing, affordable, and not overpowering. Competitive packaging also suggested the original bottle felt bulky.

Direction

A deeper purple bottle, refined typography hierarchy, removal of dense bullet points, and illustrated elements create a younger, more atmospheric shelf presence.
