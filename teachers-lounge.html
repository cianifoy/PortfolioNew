Teachers' Lounge

Teachers’ Lounge is an application where teachers help teachers. It meets educators where they are in an always-accessible digital community designed to facilitate mentorship among K–12 educators in a way that is supportive, collaborative, and approachable.

Role
Domain over discover page, mentorship matching, library, and profile creation
Team
2 UX Researchers and 1 UX Designer

1st Place Prize at the University of Michigan School of Information Exposition ‘26, in the category of Life-changing Education.

Problem

44% of new teachers exit the profession within 5 years. How might we facilitate informal mentorship for early career K-12 teachers to increase retention and job satisfaction in the field?

Approach

Rather than attempting to solve structural problems embedded in the education system, this project seeks to identify specific, addressable burdens that contribute to burnout and attrition for newly graduated K–12 teachers.

Research

We conducted a literature review, digital ethnography, interviews with early career and experienced teachers, a MaxDiff survey to prioritize features, and usability testing to gather product feedback.

Key insight

Teacher burnout is driven by administrative misalignment, lack of support, and isolation. Early career teachers need more preparation and guidance in the first months of teaching.
