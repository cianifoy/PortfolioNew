Anchor

Anchor is a centralized platform that streamlines the jobs and internship search process for University of Michigan School of Information students—bringing critical career resources into one intuitive experience.

Role
User flows (events), wireframes (events page), prototype, usability testing, and branding
Team
3 UX Designers

Problem

How might we leverage and reorganize UMSI’s resources and network to increase success finding internships and post-graduate opportunities while decreasing cognitive load for overwhelmed students?

Research

We conducted eight in-depth interviews with UMSI students across diverse academic tracks, then synthesized insights into themes and three personas that guided design decisions.

Process

Information architecture, sitemap prioritization, iterative design, and usability testing covered tasks like finding mentors, registering for events, applying through alumni referrals, and saving job listings.
